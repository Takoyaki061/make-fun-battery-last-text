# Candidates — The Battery's Last Text to Its Charger

Judge criteria (most important first): (1) reads as the battery's last text
to its charger; (2) a real joke with a twist paying off in the final words,
not just sad/cute; (3) sounds like a person actually texting — voice, mood,
neediness or spite; (4) spends its 200 characters well, setup earns the
punchline, no filler; (5) a fresh angle beyond the obvious dying-battery
complaint, funny on a second read.

Generated with DeepSeek (100milionseek, deepseek-v4.1-flash-free) in brutal
mode: two generation batches, then a DeepSeek critique round, then my own
critique-pick. All 14 candidates below; the pick is A4.

## Critique (my own words, with DeepSeek as critic)

- **A4 — PICK.** DeepSeek's #1 too. Perfectly in-voice: needy, keeping
  receipts, the jealousy is specific and mundane. The twist lands on the
  very last word ("toaster"), so the punchline is the final word. Funny
  again on re-read once you clock the read-receipts joke. 111 chars — and
  the final tie-break favours smaller text. Hits all five criteria.
- **A7 — runner-up.** Best pure spite; the reveal re-frames the whole text
  (she sat on the information). Knock: "upside down" is a small, slightly
  technical gag for a big setup — wittier than funny.
- **A1 — third.** Cleanest read-aloud; "seeing other outlets" is a genuinely
  good double meaning. Loses because the outlet pun is the obvious one —
  clever rather than surprising.
- **A5** — punchy and short, real twist at the end, but the 5W/65W gag is a
  spec joke, not a texting voice; less neediness/spite than A4.
- **A6** — good will/lamp twist, but the lamp payoff is a second joke that
  dilutes the charger address (criterion 1).
- **B3** — great line, but the will twist could be about anyone; not enough
  charger in it.
- **A3** — dense; the 87% earbuds detail muddies the cheating joke.
- **B6** — fun ("you coward"), but haunting the port is a ghost joke, not a
  last text; weaker on criterion 1.
- **A2, A8, B1, B2, B4, B5** — each has one good beat but a weaker payoff:
  A2's "emotionally regulated" is cute, not a punchline; A8's vacuum reveal
  is good but the setup is long; B1's power bank is a decent twist but the
  setup drags; B2's airport USB is a good image with a flat ending; B4's
  airplane-mode pun lands mid-line, not at the end; B5's "you are the cycle"
  is clever wordplay, not a texting voice.

## Pick

**A4** — "I sent you twelve little lightning bolts today. Twelve. You opened every single one and replied to the toaster." (111 chars)
